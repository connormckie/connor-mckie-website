Connor McKie

Connor McKie is a hard techno, hard groove, and hard trance DJ and producer from Chester, UK. Warehouse pressure, driving kicks, sets built to cut a dark room.

Time in Taiwan put him in rooms that move: Reboot Taipei, Techno Bus, an abandoned rave with Hendou Music and Critical Dose, La Fin. Originals go out as Connor McKie. This page is the solo project — not the duo.
